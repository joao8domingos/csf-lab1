        <h2>Protagonists &amp; Story</h2>
        <p>The game follows <strong>Lucia</strong> and <strong>Jason</strong>, a couple navigating Vice City's criminal underworld. The narrative draws inspiration from the real-life Bonnie and Clyde, with themes of loyalty, betrayal, and the American Dream gone wrong. Players can switch between both characters freely, similar to GTA V's character-switching mechanic but with deeper relationship dynamics that affect mission outcomes and dialogue.</p>
        <p>Supporting characters include a diverse cast of drug lords, corrupt politicians, crypto scammers, and social media influencers : reflecting Rockstar's signature satirical take on modern American culture.</p>
        <h2>Gameplay Details</h2>
        <p>Rockstar has confirmed the following gameplay innovations:</p>
        <ul>
            <li><strong>Advanced NPC AI:</strong> Every NPC has a daily routine, relationships with other NPCs, and reacts dynamically to player actions. NPCs remember past interactions.</li>
            <li><strong>Enhanced Wanted System:</strong> Police response now factors in witness reports, CCTV cameras, and license plate recognition. Evading the law requires more strategic planning.</li>
            <li><strong>Interior Exploration:</strong> Over 70% of buildings now feature accessible interiors, including apartments, offices, malls, and underground facilities.</li>
            <li><strong>Vehicle Customization 2.0:</strong> A rebuilt customization system with per-part physics simulation, engine tuning that affects performance curves, and cosmetic options exceeding 10,000 combinations per vehicle.</li>
            <li><strong>Economy Overhaul:</strong> A dynamic in-game economy where stock prices, property values, and commodity prices fluctuate based on player actions and world events.</li>
        </ul>

        <h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
        <p><strong>Q: Will GTA VI have an online mode?</strong><br>
        A: Yes. Rockstar has confirmed that GTA Online 2.0 will launch alongside the single-player campaign, featuring a persistent world with dedicated servers, cross-play between PlayStation and Xbox, and a completely rebuilt anti-cheat system.</p>
        <p><strong>Q: Can I transfer my GTA Online character?</strong><br>
        A: Rockstar has not confirmed character transfers. Industry insiders suggest a fresh start is more likely, though returning players may receive loyalty bonuses and exclusive cosmetics.</p>
        <p><strong>Q: Will there be microtransactions?</strong><br>
        A: Given the billions generated by Shark Cards in GTA Online, microtransactions are virtually guaranteed for the online component. Single-player is expected to remain free of paid content.</p>
        <p><strong>Q: Is the September 2022 leak build playable?</strong><br>
        A: The leaked build was an early development version from 2021, heavily incomplete and not representative of the final product. It is not publicly distributed and pursuing it may result in legal action from Take-Two Interactive.</p>
        <p><strong>Q: Will the game feature NFTs or blockchain integration?</strong><br>
        A: No official announcements suggest NFT integration. Given the industry backlash against blockchain gaming features, it is unlikely Rockstar will pursue this direction. Multiple job listings at Rockstar have explicitly stated "no web3 or blockchain experience required," further suggesting the company is not exploring this space. The gaming community has largely rejected play-to-earn mechanics, and Rockstar's brand positioning as a premium single-player and multiplayer experience makes blockchain integration counterproductive to their business model. Analysts at Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley, and JPMorgan have all published notes indicating that AAA publishers are moving away from blockchain experiments after the failures of Ubisoft Quartz, EA's NFT explorations, and Square Enix's Symbiogenesis underperformance. The total addressable market for blockchain gaming in 2026 is estimated at only 0.3% of the traditional gaming market, making the ROI proposition unfavorable for publishers of Rockstar's scale. Furthermore, the European Union's Markets in Crypto-Assets (MiCA) regulation, which came into full effect in December 2024, imposes strict requirements on digital asset offerings that would add significant compliance overhead to any in-game token economy. Take-Two Interactive's CEO Strauss Zelnick has publicly stated on multiple earnings calls that the company is "skeptical" of NFT integration in games and believes "the value proposition for consumers remains unclear." Given these factors, the probability of GTA VI incorporating any form of blockchain technology is estimated at less than 0.1% by industry analysts. Rockstar's focus remains on delivering a premium narrative experience with a robust online companion, following the proven GTA V model that has generated over 8 billion dollars in revenue since 2013 without any web3 integration whatsoever.</p>
